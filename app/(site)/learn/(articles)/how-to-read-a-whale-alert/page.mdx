# How to read a whale alert

An alert in Telegram looks roughly like this:

> **Smart money buys — SOL**
> Wallet: Fund A (7xKX…gAsU)
> Bought: 12.4M $BONK (~$84,200) with 120 $SOL
> Solscan · DexScreener

## Line by line

- **The headline** says what happened and on which chain: buys, sells, transfer, deposit to or withdrawal from an exchange.
- **Wallet** is the label we gave the address, followed by a shortened address. Click through on the website to see every transaction of that wallet.
- **The amount** is shown in tokens and in dollars. The dollar value uses the price at the moment we saw the transaction, not the moment of the transaction itself.
- **With** or **for** shows what was on the other side of a swap.
- **The links** go to the transaction in the explorer (Etherscan or Solscan) and to the token on DexScreener.

## Why you don't see every transaction

We only post what is above a threshold, and we skip tokens with very little liquidity, because that is often spam. When one wallet makes many swaps in a short time, we bundle them into one message so your group doesn't get flooded.

## What an alert is not

An alert is not a tip. It's a fact: this wallet did this. What it means depends on context we don't know. Want more background on a token? Use <code>/analyze</code> in the group.
